## Research rules
1. Prefer original sources: regulators, government agencies, company announcements, court/filing documents, official data releases and named industry associations.
2. Use reputable trade publications for context and reporting.
3. Use social posts and forums as signals, not proof.
4. Confirm dates, geography, sample size, methodology and definitions for statistics.
5. Cross-check important claims with a second independent source where possible.
6. Link the original source, not only a search result.
7. Keep the publication date distinct from the date the event occurred.
8. If a source is inaccessible, say so. Never pretend to have scraped it.
9. Respect robots.txt, site terms, paywalls, rate limits and copyright. Prefer RSS, APIs, search indexing or permitted access over aggressive scraping.
10. Do not copy article text. Summarise in original language and quote only short excerpts when necessary.

## Guest-post opportunity evaluation
Score each prospect on:
- Audience match (title, mortgage, insurance, real estate, property data, PropTech)
- Editorial credibility and real readership evidence
- Topic fit and likely reader value
- Author/contributor policy
- Link policy and transparency
- Recent publication activity
- Evidence of genuine editorial review
- Outreach route and named editor only if publicly verified

Do not call a site a “high-authority opportunity” based on domain metrics alone.
