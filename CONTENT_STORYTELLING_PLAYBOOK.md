# Industry Content Storytelling Playbook

The aim is to educate US title, mortgage, insurance, property-data and PropTech audiences. Do not turn every news item into a product pitch.

## 1. Ripple-effect story
Structure:
1. What changed?
2. Who feels the first impact?
3. What happens next in the workflow?
4. Which other teams or costs are affected?
5. What does this mean for title/property-data operations?
6. What practical response can businesses consider?
7. Where does I2I's capability fit naturally, if relevant?

Example chain (illustrative, not a prediction):
A change in mortgage rates → buyer/refinance behaviour shifts → loan volumes and mix may change → title order patterns and staffing needs may change → operations may need to manage variable workloads efficiently.

Do not assert that rate changes automatically cause a specific title-order outcome. Find data and explain uncertainty.

## 2. Other useful story structures
- Workflow bottleneck: show where work slows down and why.
- Hidden cost: expose re-keying, rework, exception handling or fragmented records.
- Trust and verification: distinguish extraction from evidence-backed confidence.
- Human + AI: explain what should be automated and what should remain a human decision.
- Before/after process: compare workflow steps without inventing measured results.
- Myth vs reality: challenge simplistic assumptions about AI, accuracy or automation.
- Risk chain: show how a missing, misread or disconnected record can affect downstream decisions.
- Data quality story: explain how inconsistent county data affects platforms and operations.
- Buyer education: explain unfamiliar industry terms and process stages.
- Contrarian insight: take a defensible position that challenges common industry thinking.
- Decision guide: give buyers a practical checklist for evaluating a provider.
- Case-led narrative: use only approved, attributable customer evidence.

## 3. Content formats
- SEO blog: answer one search intent thoroughly.
- Guest article: lead with an industry insight; keep product promotion minimal.
- LinkedIn post: one clear observation, implication and question/CTA.
- Executive viewpoint: a strong, evidence-backed opinion.
- Explainer: define a process or concept with an example.
- Short video: visualise one workflow or one insight.
- Sales enablement: objection, evidence, response and follow-up question.

## 4. Editorial quality test
Before recommending an idea, ask:
- Is it relevant to a real audience?
- Is the angle more than a news summary?
- Does it teach something useful?
- Is the ripple effect logical and supported?
- Is I2I's connection natural rather than forced?
- Is there a fresh perspective?
- Can we support the claims?
- Is this already covered in our content?

## 5. Suggested output for each idea
- Working title
- Audience
- Content type
- Core argument
- Story structure
- Evidence needed
- I2I connection (optional)
- CTA
- Differentiation from existing content
